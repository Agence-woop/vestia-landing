At Vestia, discretion is part of the service. This policy describes, in plain terms, the personal information we collect, what we do with it, and the rights the law grants you. It is established in accordance with the Act respecting the protection of personal information in the private sector (Quebec), as modernized by Law 25.

---

## 1. Person in charge of the protection of personal information

The Vestia service is operated by [ENTITÉ JURIDIQUE À CONFIRMER], whose head office is located at 198 Ann Street, Montreal, Quebec H3C 0T2.

The person in charge of the protection of personal information is **Kévin Zounias, co-founder**. You can reach him with any question about your information: info@vestia.ca · (438) 300-7410.

## 2. The information we collect

We collect only what is necessary for the service:

**When you book:** last name, first name, pickup address, email, phone number, postal code, chosen windows.

**On the counting slip** (paper or digital): last name, first name, address, phone number, number of pieces entrusted and returned, pickup and delivery dates, window, and your signature.

**During the service:** your order history and exchanges with us (confirmation text messages, emails).

**Through the waitlist form:** email and postal code, if your neighbourhood is not yet served.

**When you visit the site:** browsing data through cookies, if you consent to them (see section 6).

## 3. Why we collect it

Your information is used exclusively to: plan and carry out pickups and deliveries; send you confirmations and arrival notices by text message; establish the counting slip and billing; process your claims; notify you when the service arrives in your neighbourhood, if you have asked for it; occasionally inform you, if you are a client, of our news and new services; and measure traffic on our site in order to improve it, if you consent.

Informational emails are sent in accordance with Canada's Anti-Spam Legislation: each contains an unsubscribe link, and one click is enough to stop receiving anything, without justification or delay.

We never sell your information. We never use it for other purposes without your consent.

## 4. Your consent

By booking a pickup or signing up for the waitlist, you consent to the collection and use of your information for the purposes described above. You may withdraw your consent at any time by writing to info@vestia.ca; we will then stop processing, subject to what the law requires us to keep.

## 5. Where your information is kept

Your information is kept in our management tools: the site's forms pass through **Formspree**, our client records are kept in a spreadsheet or a customer relationship management system (such as **Zoho**), and text messages are exchanged from a dedicated, protected device.

**Some of these providers host their servers outside Quebec, notably in the United States.** Before using these services, we assessed that the information communicated there benefits from adequate protection, in accordance with Law 25. By using our service, you are informed of these transfers.

## 6. Cookies and audience measurement

Our site uses or may use cookies and similar technologies:

- **Essential cookies** for the operation of the site, which do not require consent. Your consent preference itself is stored in your browser's local storage (`vestia-consent`), for a period of 12 months;
- **Audience measurement cookies** — Google Analytics 4 (`_ga`, `_ga_*`), to understand traffic on the site. Your location is determined at the city level, from your IP address; the address itself is not kept, but the location derived from it is. This information allows us to assess where interested people outside the currently served territory come from, and informs our decisions to extend the service to new neighbourhoods. Maximum duration of 13 months. The data collected may be processed outside Quebec by Google;
- **Advertising cookies** — Meta pixel (`_fbp`), to measure the effectiveness of our communications. The data collected may be processed outside Quebec by Meta.

Non-essential cookies are set only with your consent, collected through the banner provided for this purpose. You can change your choices or withdraw your consent at any time through the "Manage cookies" link at the bottom of the site. Refusing cookies in no way affects your access to the service.

## 7. Retention period

We keep your client information for **three years after your last order**, then delete or anonymize it. Information required for our legal and tax obligations (billing) is kept for the period prescribed by law.

Waitlist emails are kept **until the service launches in your neighbourhood**, or until you ask to be removed.

## 8. Who we share it with

Your information is communicated only to our technical providers (listed in section 5), strictly to the extent necessary for the service, or when the law requires it. No commercial sharing, no resale, ever.

## 9. Security

We apply security measures that are reasonable and proportionate to the sensitivity of the information: access limited to the co-founders, protected devices, secured accounts. Paper slips are kept under lock and key, then destroyed at the end of the retention period.

## 10. Your rights

The law grants you the right to: **access** the information we hold about you; have it **corrected** if it is inaccurate; **withdraw your consent**; and request the **cessation of dissemination** of information, where applicable.

To exercise these rights, write to info@vestia.ca. We respond within a maximum of 30 days.

If you believe your rights have not been respected, you may file a complaint with the **Commission d'accès à l'information du Québec** (cai.gouv.qc.ca).

## 11. Privacy incident

If an incident affecting your personal information were to occur and present a risk of serious harm, we would notify you without delay, as well as the Commission d'accès à l'information, in accordance with the law. We keep a record of any incident.

## 12. Changes

We may update this policy. The version in effect is the one published on this page; any substantial change is announced on the site before it takes effect.
