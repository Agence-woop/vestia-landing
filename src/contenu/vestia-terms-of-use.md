## 2. The service

Vestia offers an ironing service with home pickup and delivery. Your clothes are picked up at your door, ironed by hand, then returned on hangers and under a protective cover.

The service is offered Monday to Friday, in the Griffintown, Cité du Multimédia, Old Montreal and Old Port neighbourhoods of Montreal. An address's eligibility is confirmed by its postal code at the time of booking.

The service is intended for persons aged 18 or over.

## 3. The pieces we accept

We process everyday garments: shirts, blouses, pants, jeans, skirts, polos, dresses, sweaters, camisoles, leggings, pleated pieces, suit jackets and similar garments, according to the price grid displayed on the site.

We respectfully decline:

- wool, cashmere and loose-knit or handmade knits;
- haute couture and designer pieces;
- coats and trench coats;
- leather and suede.

**Pieces must be given to us clean.** Vestia offers an ironing service, not a washing service. We reserve the right to refuse at pickup any piece that is soiled, unwashed, shows pre-existing damage or belongs to the excluded categories; the refused piece is then simply removed from the slip, at no charge.

## 4. Booking

Booking is done online. Each order includes a minimum of eight pieces; every pickup is billed at a minimum of $48, corresponding to eight simple pieces, even if fewer pieces are entrusted.

You may change or cancel your pickup at no charge until 6 p.m. on the day before the agreed date. In the event of late cancellation or absence at pickup time, no fee is charged at this time; Vestia nevertheless reserves the possibility of later applying a travel fee, which would then be announced before any booking.

## 5. The joint count

At pickup, every piece is counted with you and recorded on a slip, paper or digital, which you initial. At return, the pieces are recounted with you and you sign the slip, which attests to the complete delivery of the order.

**The signed slip is authoritative as to the number of pieces entrusted and returned.** No claim regarding the number of pieces can be received after the return slip has been signed.

## 6. Timelines and the return

Your pickup window is an agreed appointment, not an estimate.

The return time is chosen by you at the time of booking, from the options offered, then confirmed by text message. Pieces picked up in the morning can come back as early as the same evening. Pieces picked up at the end of the day come back no earlier than the evening of the next business day. You may also choose a later return, or arrange a time with us.

Returns take place between 5:30 and 7:30 p.m. A message announces our arrival.

Vestia is firmly committed to the agreed times. They may exceptionally be suspended in cases of force majeure — extreme weather conditions, breakdown, or any event beyond our reasonable control. If so, you are notified without delay and a new time is offered to you as a priority.

## 7. Delivery

Delivery is made in person at the pickup address, during the agreed window.

If you are absent at delivery time, a new delivery is scheduled for the next business day; a redelivery fee of $5 may then apply. With your prior agreement, the order may also be handed to a third party you designate (concierge, neighbour), in which case handover to that third party constitutes delivery.

Any order unclaimed ninety (90) days after the first delivery attempt is deemed abandoned and may be given to a charitable organization.

## 8. Payment

Payment is due at the time of delivery, by Interac transfer or in cash for the exact amount — our couriers carry no change. Displayed prices are before applicable taxes, if any. Tipping remains entirely at your discretion and is never expected.

In the event of non-payment on delivery, Vestia may suspend access to the service until the situation is resolved.

## 9. Our liability

Vestia handles every piece with the care of an atelier. If a piece were nevertheless damaged or lost through our fault, our liability is limited to ten times the processing price of the piece concerned, as displayed in the price grid at the time of the order.

Any claim for damage must be sent to us within 24 hours of delivery, with photographs, to info@vestia.ca or by text message at (438) 300-7410 — including on weekends. Claims received on weekends are recorded upon receipt and processed on the next business day; if the claim is founded, the compensation applies. The piece concerned must be available for us to examine.

Vestia cannot be held responsible for items left in garments (pockets, linings). Any item found is gladly returned at delivery.

Nothing in these terms limits the rights conferred on you by the legal warranties and the mandatory provisions of the Consumer Protection Act (Quebec).
